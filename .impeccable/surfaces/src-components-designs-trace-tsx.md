## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is one vital-sign trace — trial velocity drawn as a single continuous cyan line — and every section hangs off it. Refuses the category default: dark SaaS hero with cyan glow and card grids.
OWN-WORLD: White ground with a faint slate chart-paper grid only in the hero; slate-900 ink, slate-500/600 secondary; one cyan-500 trace and cyan-500 pill buttons with slate-950 text; Inter; no cards, boxes or icon tiles — hierarchy is position on the line. Dark slate only in the footer.
STORY: Visitor reads the headline, watches the trace draw like a monitor waking, understands "live signal", scrolls down the line through metrics, pillars, features, audiences, governance, and reaches the form where the line ends.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned headline ~5rem across 8 columns; full-bleed trace drawing left→right beneath it with a live dot at its head; sub-headline and CTAs below; trust line at the bottom edge. Primary CTA sits directly under the sub-headline.
FORM: Vital-sign monitor trace, candidate 3 of 7 grounded list; seed key 531b5440.
RAISE (from drum-machine step row): the trace is the page's one unbroken line, and a "now" marker rides it tracking scroll position.
RAISE (from cracktro scroller): nothing enclosed — no rules around content, no cards; text rides baselines off the line.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: hero trace draws on load (stroke-dashoffset), then the vertical spine line fills and its node dots light as each section passes the reading line.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
